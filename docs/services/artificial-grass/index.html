Turf

A good turf install is mostly what's underneath.

Artificial grass solves the spots real grass struggles with: shade, heavy foot traffic, dogs, narrow side yards and yards you just don't want to mow. We install natural-looking, North American made turf for front yards, backyards and side strips across Edmonton.

The difference between turf that looks great for years and turf that ripples, puddles and smells is the base. We excavate, build a compacted gravel base that drains, then seam and secure the turf so it stays tight, level and clean.

Love golf? We also build custom backyard putting greens, installed on the same solid base, that roll true and make the perfect centrepiece for an entertaining space.

What's included

  • Excavation and removal of existing grass
  • Compacted, permeable gravel base
  • North American made turf, seamed and secured
  • Custom putting greens with cups and flags
  • Concrete curb or paver edging for a clean line
  • 1-year coverage on turf installation

Best forShady yards, busy family and dog yards, side strips and anyone done with mowing.

How we build it

Done properly, in the right order.

  1. 01

    Excavate

    We remove the existing grass and soil to the right depth so the turf sits flush with your edges.

  2. 02

    Base and drainage

    A gravel base is placed, levelled and compacted so water drains straight through.

  3. 03

    Lay and seam

    Turf is rolled out, seamed invisibly and secured around every edge.

  4. 04

    Infill and brush

    Infill is spread and brushed in so the blades stand up and the lawn looks natural.

Artificial turf questions

Straight answers.

Something we didn't cover? Call 780-940-8283, 9 am to 5 pm, 7 days a week.

Does artificial turf work in Edmonton winters?

Yes. Turf on a properly draining base handles freeze-thaw well, and it's green again the moment the snow melts, weeks before a real lawn wakes up.

Is it good for dogs?

Yes. The permeable base lets liquid drain through, and the surface rinses clean with a hose. We'll recommend the right turf and infill for pets.

How long does artificial grass last?

Quality turf installed on a solid base typically lasts many years. The base is what keeps it looking good, which is why we never skip it.
