Best Landscaping Company in Edmonton (2026 Guide): How to Choose the Right Contractor

March 20, 2026·4 min read

Choosing a landscaping company in Edmonton isn’t just about finding someone to make your yard look good—it’s about finding a contractor who can build and maintain something that actually holds up.

With Edmonton’s weather, a poorly done job won’t last long. We’ve seen patios shift after one winter, drainage issues show up after heavy rain, and lawns struggle because the prep wasn’t done properly.

That’s why picking the right landscaping company in Edmonton matters. Done right, your outdoor space should last for years—not need fixing every season.

At Buksh Landscapes, we focus on building and maintaining outdoor spaces that are practical, durable, and built for real conditions.

Why Choosing the Right Landscaping Company Matters

A landscape isn’t just something you look at—it’s something that has to perform.

A lot of the repair jobs we come across are the result of shortcuts:

  • Poor grading causing water to pool near foundations
  • Patios sinking because the base wasn’t compacted properly
  • Retaining walls failing due to lack of drainage
  • High-maintenance yards that weren’t planned properly

On the flip side, working with a professional landscaping company in Edmonton means:

  • Proper final grading and drainage from the start
  • Solid base prep for patios and hardscaping
  • Efficient, consistent maintenance
  • Reliable snow removal through the winter

In Edmonton, durability isn’t optional—it’s everything.

What Actually Makes a Landscaping Company “The Best”?

There’s a big difference between companies that just do the work and ones that do it properly.

1. They Handle Both Construction and Maintenance

A company that builds and maintains landscape services and understands how everything holds up over time.

That means better decisions during the build phase—and fewer issues later.

2. They Offer Full-Service Work

The best landscaping companies don’t just do one thing. They handle:

  • Landscape construction
  • Artificial turf installation
  • Lawn and property maintenance
  • Snow removal

This keeps everything consistent and avoids dealing with multiple contractors.

3. They Understand the Ground Work

This is where most jobs go wrong.

Experienced crews focus on:

  • Proper grading
  • Base preparation
  • Drainage planning
  • Using the right materials

That’s what determines whether your project lasts a couple years—or a couple decades.

4. They’re Reliable

This matters more than people think—especially for commercial properties.

Showing up on schedule, keeping things clean, and staying consistent is what separates professionals from everyone else.

Key Landscaping Services to Look For

When hiring a landscaping contractor in Edmonton, make sure they cover both build and maintenance.

Landscape Construction & Hardscaping

This is the foundation of any outdoor space:

  • Paver patios
  • Retaining walls
  • Final grading
  • Sod installation
  • Tree installation
  • Landscape lighting

If these are done properly, everything else becomes easier to maintain.

Artificial Turf Installation

We’ve seen a big increase in artificial turf in Edmonton, especially on:

  • High-traffic residential yards
  • Commercial properties
  • Areas where natural grass struggles

When installed properly, turf gives you:

  • Low maintenance
  • Clean, consistent look
  • Long-term durability

The key is proper base prep and drainage—otherwise it won’t perform the way it should.

Commercial Lawn Maintenance

For larger properties, consistency is everything.

Services usually include:

  • Scheduled mowing
  • Property upkeep
  • Seasonal clean-ups

A well-maintained site not only looks better—it’s easier to manage long-term.

Commercial Snow Removal

Winter is part of the job in Edmonton.

A good contractor will offer:

  • Parking lot clearing
  • Commercial snow removal
  • Reliable scheduling during storms

This keeps your property safe and operational all season.

How to Choose the Right Landscaping Contractor in Edmonton

Here’s what actually matters when making a decision:

Look at Real Work

Not just photos—look for actual patios, turf installs, and grading jobs they’ve completed.

Don’t Choose Based on Price Alone

Cheaper work often means shortcuts—and those show up later.

Ask About Their Process

A solid contractor should clearly explain:

  • How they prep the site
  • How they build the base
  • What materials they use
  • What to expect long-term

Make Sure They Can Handle Your Type of Property

A small backyard and a large commercial site are completely different. Experience with scale matters.

What We’re Seeing More of in Edmonton (2026)

From the work we’re doing, a few trends stand out:

  • More demand for artificial turf
  • Simpler, low-maintenance designs
  • Focus on usable outdoor spaces (patios, seating areas)
  • Planning for year-round use, including snow removal

People are moving away from high-maintenance landscaping and toward practical solutions.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

We see these all the time:

  • Skipping proper grading
  • Choosing based on price only
  • Hiring companies without build experience
  • Overcomplicating the design
  • Not planning for maintenance

Fixing these later usually costs more than doing it right upfront.

Final Thoughts

Finding the right landscaping company in Edmonton isn’t about picking whoever is cheapest—it’s about finding a contractor who knows how to build something that lasts.

Whether you’re installing a patio, upgrading to artificial turf, or maintaining a large property, doing it properly from the start saves time, money, and stress.
